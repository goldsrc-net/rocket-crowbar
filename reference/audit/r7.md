## Unclear, for Steven

Five rows, four decisions. Each is kept as halflife-updated has it until you pick, and listed under
DEVIATIONS.md's "Kept until Steven decides".

- **env_sound room type** (r4 `CEnvSound::Think` 0x214760, r1 `CBasePlayer::Spawn` 0x1f1000):
  halflife-updated sends the room type from UpdateClientData, resends it on spawn and full update,
  and resets it to 0 at each new map; the original sends SVC_ROOMTYPE once and its effect carries
  into the next map. Choice: keep (mod-rc2 kept the same arrangement; the carry-over reads as a
  bug, and only shows when the last map ended in an env_sound zone). Reverting means
  CEnvSound::Think writing SVC_ROOMTYPE again and dropping m_ClientSndRoomtype from player.cpp.
- **SVC_DIRECTOR damage event** (r1 `CBasePlayer::TakeDamage` 0x1ea2c0): only an HLTV proxy's
  auto-director sees it, no player. Choice: keep.
- **AmmoPickup after WeapPickup** (r1 `CBasePlayer::AddPlayerItem` 0x1f3e90): the HUD pickup
  history lists a new weapon's ammo after the weapon, the original before. Choice: revert, as
  mod-rc2's a6 did: `CBasePlayerWeapon::AddToPlayer` calls `ExtractAmmo(this)` at its end and
  AddPlayerItem drops its own call (safe now that GiveAmmo no longer gives the exhaustible weapon).
- **func_tank NaN aim** (r6 `CFuncTank::AdjustAnglesForBarrel` 0x18eea0): with the target nearer
  than the barrel offset the original takes sqrt of a negative and aims with NaN angles; ours skips
  the correction. Choice: keep as a guard (NaN angles sent to the engine are a crash risk, which
  policy guards).
