## For DEVIATIONS.md

- Nothing new. The two `engine` rows (`CBasePlayerItem::FallThink`, `DefaultTouch`) are
  halflife-updated's `sv_busters` code, which the "Cvars" entry already covers (off by default,
  so as the original). `CBasePlayerWeapon::DefaultDeploy` keeps halflife-updated's
  `TabulateAmmo()` and `m_flLastFireTime = 0`, which nothing reads in this server; not worth an
  entry unless `CLIENT_WEAPONS` ever comes back to the server.

## For ORIGINAL_BUGS.md

- `doors.cpp CMomentaryDoor::Use` (SDK 2.1 bug, kept): the moving sound starts and is never
  stopped, so a `momentary_door` keeps looping its move sound after it stops. halflife-updated
  fixed it (`DoorMoveDone`); the original has the bug.
- `doors.cpp CBaseDoor::Blocked` (SDK typo, kept): linked doors are realigned only when their
  `avelocity` equals this door's `velocity` (compares the wrong field).

## Odd

- RC 1.9 changed the door sounds: `DoorActivate`, `DoorGoUp` and `DoorGoDown` all stop the
  moving sound before playing it (the original's `EMIT_SOUND_DYN(..., 0, 0, SND_STOP, 100)`).
  SDK 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 don't, so it's RC's, probably for the stuck looping door sound. With it
  back, halflife-updated's "don't replay while moving" guard and its extra `STOP_SOUND` in
  `Blocked` are both gone.
- `FireBullets`' only caller left that the revert changes is the hgrunt shotgun
  (`BULLET_PLAYER_BUCKSHOT`, `iDamage` 0): no impact sounds or decals again, as in 2001. Every
  12mm caller passes `iTracerFreq` 1, so the `!tracer` test changes nothing for them today.
- `CMomentaryDoor::DoorMoveDone` and `StopMoveSound` are halflife-updated's and now have no
  caller; left in place (the class declares them, removing them touches the declaration).
- Ghidra split `CBaseEntity::FireBullets`, `TraceBleed`, `CCrowbar::Swing`, `CLight::KeyValue`,
  `CBaseDoor::KeyValue`, and parts of the crossbow into `FUN_` fragments. `FireBullets` was read in
  the disassembly; `TraceBleed`'s diff is style only and its body wasn't compared line by line.
- `~/stignored/rc19-tools/audit/three.py` was rewritten by another package while this one ran;
  `three-r3.py` is this package's copy.
